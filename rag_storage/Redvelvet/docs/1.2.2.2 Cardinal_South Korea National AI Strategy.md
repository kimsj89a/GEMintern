Ministry of Science and ICT  
 
 
 
 September 26, 2024 12:00 KST 
 
Presenting Blueprint for AI Innovation to Propel 
South Korea into the Top 3 Global AI Nations (AI G3) 
 
− 
In a joint effort with relevant ministries, the 1st National Artificial Intelligence 
Committee presided over by President Yoon, unveiled the National AI Strategy Policy, 
outlining a vision for AI innovation that aims to propel South Korean into the top 3 global 
AI leading nation (AI G3). 
− 
With the vision of realizing a global AI hub by achieving AI G3, the public and private 
sectors will unite as one team to advance the four major national AI flagship 
projects. 
 
➢ (Project 1) Expand the advanced GPU by 15x, establish the National AI Computing 
Center, and support the commercialization of domestic AI semiconductors. 
➢ (Project 2) Facilitate KRW65trn investment in private AI sector over the next four years 
('24~'27), backed by favorable government policy on investments. 
➢ (Project 3) Achieve an AI adoption rate of 70% in industries and 95% in the public 
sector by 2030, through a comprehensive national AI transformation.  
➢ (Project 4) Secure AI safety and security capabilities to lead global AI governance. 
 
− 
Policy is mainly focused on four key areas of the AI ecosystem: (①Startup & Talent 
Expansion, ②Technology & Infrastructure Innovation, ③Building Inclusive & Fair 
Foundation, ④Securing Global Leadership), that form the core of AI ecosystem.  
 
Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT; Minister Yoo) announced the National AI Strategy Policy at 
the ‘1st National Artificial Intelligence Committee (National AI Committee) held at the Seoul 
Four Seasons Hotel on Sep 26 (Thursday), chaired by President Yoon. 
 
 
 


- 2 - 
 
The key initiatives for National AI Strategy Policy announced today are as follows: 
 
1. Background and Significance 
An unprecedented rapid and extensive AI technology revolution is progressing as the world 
swiftly enters the AI era. As AI now transcends technology to become a transformative force 
determining national economic security, major nations including the US, China, and EU are 
fully committed to accelerating AI innovation and building global AI leadership to secure AI 
competitiveness. 
Since its establishment, our administration has maintained AI as a core national agenda, and 
has made continuous policy efforts to strengthen AI competitiveness and lead global norms 
through presidential initiatives including the 'New York Initiative' (Sep 2022), 'Paris Initiative' 
(Jun 2023), 'Digital Rights Charter' (Sep 2023), 'AI-Semiconductor Initiative' (Apr 2024), and 'AI 
Seoul Summit' (May 2024). 
While we have established an AI SW ecosystem by developing multiple proprietary generative 
AI models* and possess world-class AI semiconductor memory and manufacturing 
competitiveness, AI innovation competition led by big tech, armed with massive capital, 
demands a paradigm shift in AI policy. 
* Ranking: US (64 models) > China (42) > Korea (11) (Source: EPOCH AI, Jul 2024) 
At this critical moment where AI will determine the next 30 years, we have established the 
National AI Strategy Policy in order to drive for AI G3, leveraging our information revolution 
and AI experience and capabilities accumulated by private and public sectors. 
 
2. Policy Paradigm Shift 
Though reviewing the achievements and limitations of the previous AI policies, and 
comprehensively analyzing the recent global AI market nature and our potential, we are 
pursuing a policy paradigm shift to maximize AI innovation capabilities and supplement 
deficient areas. First, the government will support the large-scale integration of AI 
infrastructure beyond the support during the initial period, recognizing that the private sector 
may face difficulties accomplishing this on its own. Additionally, we plan to promote 
investments and encourage private sector to actively expand AI business. Furthermore, we will 
accelerate nationwide AI transformation by establishing national AI infrastructure and support 
public-private partnership to foster and expand globally.

The National AI Strategy Policy aims realizing a global AI hub by achieving AI G3 and 
presents four major AI flagship projects as core national initiatives and policy directions 
across 4 key areas (①Startup & Talent Expansion, ②Technology & Infrastructure Innovation, 
③Building Inclusive & Fair Foundation, ④Securing Global Leadership) that form the core of the

4. Key Initiatives 
4 AI Flagship Projects 
First, Significant Expansion of National AI Computing Infrastructure 
(Over 2EF by 2030: Expand the advanced GPU capacity by 15x) 
Our industrial and research sectors currently face an absolute shortage of AI computing 
infrastructure compared to big tech companies. To prepare for the next 30 years of AI era, we 
plan to expand advanced GPU capacity by more than 15x (over 2EF by 2030) and support 
early commercialization of domestic AI semiconductors. Initially, we will establish a National 
AI Computing Center worth up to KRW2trn based on public-private joint investment and 
support private sector AI computing infrastructure expansion through low-interest loan 
programs (by 2027). This will promote continuous private investment in AI computing 
infrastructure and provide world-class AI computing infrastructure to companies and 
researchers. Additionally, we will establish the domestic AI computing ecosystem by introducing 
Korean AI semiconductors such as NPU and PIM and develop specialized HW/SW 
technologies. Furthermore, we plan to support exports and drive the globalization of the 
domestic AI computing ecosystem through collaboration with global companies. 
 
 


- 4 - 
 
Second, Substantial Increase in Private Sector AI Investment 
(Private investment of KRW65trn over 4 years (by 2027), government support on investments) 
While private investment in Korea lags behind major nations, the private sector will execute 
total investments of KRW65trn in AI over four years starting this year (2024-2027), with the 
government to promote active investments. While the private sector intensifies investment in AI 
technology, talent, and computing infrastructure, the government will review related tax support 
and expand policy financing, including large-scale project fund. This will lead to the creation 
of a virtuous investment ecosystem in overall AI value chain, enabling us to actively and 
effectively compete in the global AI arena. 
 
Third, Full-scale National AX (AI+X) Implementation  
(Achievement of 70% industry, 95% public sector AI adoption rate by 2030) 
Beyond limited AI utilization in specific fields, we will pursue nationwide AI transformation 
across industries, public sector, society, regional communities, and national defense. 
First, we will establish and implement AX (AI+X) measures for 8 key industries with high AI 
adoption potentials but slow AI transformation, including manufacturing, finance, and 
medical/bio. We will develop public sector AI services (Public AX) in 18 areas including 
safety, disaster response, and healthcare through AI utilization capabilities driven by 
government AI infrastructure implementation. Additionally, we will address employments 
changes due to AI, strengthen protections for vulnerable workers, and initiate a major public 
education transformation through AI digital textbook from 2025. Furthermore, we will establish 
regional AI innovation hubs and create/expand AI utilization in national defense by developing 
military AI security policies and expanding national defense AI infrastructure. Through this 
nationwide AI implementation, we plan to enhance AI adoption rates in industry (70%) and 
public sector (95%) by 2030, improving Korea's overall economic potential through AI to 
generate KRW310trn economic effects (as of 2026). 
 
Fourth, Securing AI Safety & Security  
(Leading Global AI Governance) 
As advanced AI-related risks like deepfake crimes and cyber threats expand globally, the 
importance of AI safety and security is being highlighted. We will establish the 'AI Safety 
Research Institute' this November as a dedicated national agency for systematic response to 
sophisticated AI risks and pursue enactment of the ‘AI Basic Act’ within this year to achieve 
balanced AI development and safety trust. Additionally, we plan to widely spread the 
normative values of the ‘Seoul Declaration’ globally and expand international cooperation for 
AI utilization in military and security fields. Furthermore, we will strengthen global AI 
leadership by supporting discovery/expansion of cooperation models between domestic and 
global companies, establishing global AI international joint research platforms including ‘Global 
AI Frontier Lab’, and actively pursuing ‘Global AI ODA Projects’.

Policy Directions across 4 Key Areas 
First, Startup & Talent Expansion 
(Strengthen foundational capabilities supporting national AI innovation)  
While global AI unicorn companies continue to emerge, domestic AI unicorn companies are still 
absent, and shortage in AI talent pool remains. Accordingly, we will pursue nurturing 10 AI 
unicorn companies by 2030 through supporting foundation of specialized AI startups and 
establishing an environment for active M&As, while securing 200,000 AI talents by 2030 
(51,000 as of 2023) through streamlining, globalizing, and broadening AI talent development. 
 
Second, Technology & Infrastructure Innovation  
(Expanding Core AI Technologies and Pursuing AI Infrastructure Innovation)  
Initially, we will expand technological cooperation with leading nations through establishing 
and operating AI-semiconductor joint research cooperation hubs, and pursue securing 
competitiveness in on-device AI, an emerging AI market. Additionally, we will support AI 
industry innovation by reforming personal information regulations and establishing data 
utilization frameworks tailored for generative AI. Furthermore, we will pursue advancement 
of AI networks through low-latency, low-power Edge network innovation and respond to 
increasing AI traffic and power demands through enactment of the ‘National Power Grid 
Special Act’. 
 
Third, Building Inclusive & Fair Foundation  
(Creating Foundations for Sustainable AI Development and Diffusion) 
We will pursue enactment of the ‘Digital Inclusion Act’ to ensure public access and 
utilization to AI so everyone can leverage AI. Furthermore, we will establish fair competition 
order and legitimate rights protection foundations supporting AI innovation. Additionally, we 
plan to develop measures preventing unfair discrimination and privacy infringement in AI 
development and utilization processes. 
 
Fourth, Securing Global Leadership  
(Establishing New AI Era Order and Leading Global AI Norms and Governance)  
We will establish fundamental legal principles addressing new issues of the AI era, such as AI 
accountability and rights attribution. We will develop protection measures for individuals and 
vulnerable groups in the age of AI-driven automated decision making. Furthermore, as a leading 
global AI nation, we plan to continuously expand AI cooperation with other leading countries 
and international organizations, while actively engaging in discussions on AI governance.

5. Implementation Structure 
The 'National AI Committee' launched today plans to establish and implement the 'National AI 
Strategy' that concretizes the ‘National AI Strategy Policy’ into policy tasks. In particular, the 
'National AI Committee' will operate subcommittees involving top experts and relevant ministries 
in each field to derive detailed tasks and implementation plans by area, and systematically 
support 'National AI Committee' activities through support group operation. 
President Yoon, who presided over the meeting as chairman of the 'National AI Committee', 
declared “With the grand vision of leaping South Korea into AI G3, we are launching a National 
all-out effort to achieve this goal.”  
He emphasized that "the newly established 'National AI Committee' will serve as the focal 
point for public-private cooperation, harnessing nationwide innovation capabilities. It will lead 
the critical AI transition at the forefront, as we collectively design and realize the AI-driven 
future of the next 30 years."
